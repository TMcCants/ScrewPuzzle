# Unscrew Wonders: worldwide launch scope

User confirmed worldwide launch on 2026-10-20. This supersedes the provisional US-first assumption in the refinement notes.

## Findings and limitations

The completed US exact-phrase and combined screw/wonder stem searches remain preliminary evidence only. See UNSCREW_WONDERS_REFINEMENT.md for exact queries and results.

Additional exact-name web searches, including searches restricted to EUIPO, UK IPO, Canadian and Australian official domains, did not surface an exact-name record. Search-engine indexing is not a substitute for searching those registers directly; those jurisdictions are NOT cleared.

WIPO Global Brand Database direct browser access failed with ERR_BLOCKED_BY_CLIENT. No international database search result was obtained. An earlier web attempt encountered a challenge. Do not interpret either failure as no matches.

## Scope needed before official branding

- International and national/regional register searches, including confusingly similar spellings, sounds, meanings and relevant translations, for game software and entertainment services.
- Country-specific common-law/unregistered use checks where relevant, plus storefront and commercial-use checks.
- Professional assessment of potentially related marks, including the US records already identified.
- Prioritize US, EU, UK, Canada and Australia as an initial review group, then cover the remaining intended distribution territories; that initial group is not worldwide clearance.
- Determine protection/filing strategy separately from availability screening. A Madrid application does not automatically provide rights everywhere.
